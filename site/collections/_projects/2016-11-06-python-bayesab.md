A small Bayesian A/B testing framework for **conversion experiments**: hierarchical Beta–Bernoulli models, pairwise rate differences (δ), and MCMC sampling via PyMC3. Supply per-variant exposure counts `n` and conversion counts `c`, then inspect posteriors and trace plots.
