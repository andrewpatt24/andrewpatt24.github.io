I'm Andy Patterson, a Staff Data Scientist based in the UK. On paper that's a formal title; in practice it means I spend a lot of time on machine learning, experimentation, and the kind of technical leadership that helps teams ship useful models—not just notebooks.

My background is in modelling complex user behaviour: how people respond to product changes, pricing, and marketing, and what that means for revenue, retention, and profitability. I'm happiest when the work connects analysis to decisions—whether that's forecasting, time-series work, classification, or building out test frameworks and measurement so we trust what we learn from an A/B test.

I've worked across the stack enough to care about the boring-but-important bits: validation and cross-validation, sensible targeting strategies, deployment (including Azure), and turning prototypes into something maintainable. I use R heavily and I'm comfortable with the usual ML toolkit—think gradient boosting (e.g. LightGBM), linear models, clustering when it fits the problem, and plenty of exploratory analysis before anyone commits to a fancy algorithm.

I also get a lot of energy from mentoring, helping analysts and data scientists grow, and from the unglamorous work of process, documentation, and oversight that keeps data work legible to the rest of the business.

If you would like to get in touch, connect with me on LinkedIn.
